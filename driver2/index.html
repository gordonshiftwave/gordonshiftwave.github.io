Ukrainian Rescue Drivers and Shiftwave

During the war in Ukraine, volunteer drivers carried civilians out of areas under attack, often on very long runs with little rest. Within 6 weeks of the full-scale invasion, the Shiftwave team began donating Shiftwaves to evacuation rescue drivers. In this video, Pavlo, a volunteer bus driver, and a rescue driver at a Kidsave rescue center near Vinnytsia describe using Shiftwave, including after trips lasting 20 hours.
